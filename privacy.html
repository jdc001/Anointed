Anointed

Privacy policy · effective 15 November 2026

Who we are

Anointed is made and published by Loveseyland Ltd., Toronto, Ontario, Canada ("we"). This policy covers the Anointed app for iPhone and iPad and the Anointed website at jdc001.github.io/Anointed. Questions about privacy go to lovesey@loveseyland.com.

The short version

Anointed collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party software development kits. Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to send it, and the only thing we ever receive is a bug report you send yourself.

What stays on your device

Your campaigns, save slots, settings, trophies, rank, the record of your past games, and whether you have bought the full game or an expansion are stored on your device, in the game's own storage. In the iPhone and iPad app, the game also keeps a copy of the same information in a file in the app's private folder, so it can be put back if the game's storage is ever cleared. That file stays on your device. If you back up your device to iCloud or to a computer, Apple's backup includes it, under your own Apple account. We cannot see it.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

What you can choose to send

  • Backups and save files. If you make a backup or export a save file or save code, it goes only where you send it: Files, iCloud Drive, a message, an email. We do not receive it.
  • Pictures of results. If you share a result card, it goes only where you choose.
  • Bug reports. Only if you tap Report a bug and then Send, the game sends us a report containing: the game version; your device's browser identifier (which names the device type and operating system version) and screen size; your language and game settings; your last moves and the game's recent chronicle; any error messages; anything you type in the note box; and, if you leave the box ticked, your current game. A report contains no name, account, location, advertising identifier or contact details unless you type them into the note. Reports are stored in a Google Sheet that we control, through Google Apps Script, and Google processes them on our behalf as a service provider. We use them only to find and fix problems, and we delete them once the problem is fixed or after 12 months, whichever comes first.

Purchases

In the app, the full game and the expansion maps are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy. We never see your name, Apple ID, payment details or address; the App Store only tells the game which purchases your Apple account owns, so it can unlock them and restore them.

The website

The website is served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives the technical details of each request (such as your IP address) under GitHub's own privacy policy. The website loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so Google receives a similar request when the page first loads. The iPhone and iPad app carries its typefaces inside the app and does not make these requests. The website sets no cookies.

Children

Anointed is a strategy game for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age. If you believe a child has sent us personal information in a bug report note, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal information apart from bug reports you choose to send, there is usually nothing for us to give you, correct or delete. If you have sent a bug report and want it deleted, or want to know what it contained, write to us at the address above and we will act within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights under laws such as Canada's PIPEDA, the GDPR in the European Union and the United Kingdom, or California's CCPA, and you can complain to your local data protection authority. We do not sell or share personal information, as those laws define it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If a change ever means the game would collect something it does not collect today, we will say so in the app before it happens.

Support

For help, or to report a problem, use Report a bug in the game's Settings, email lovesey@loveseyland.com, or open an issue at github.com/jdc001/Anointed/issues. Say which version you are on (Settings shows it at the bottom) and what you were doing.
